# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-13

- First complete Tokyo miniature, built from scratch.
- Compressed geography: Tokyo Bay, Sumida River and five crossings, Imperial Palace moat, Odaiba,
  Ueno Park, Meiji forest, Shiba Park and the Sumida promenade.
- Eight landmarks: Tokyo Tower, Tokyo Skytree, Senso-ji, Rainbow Bridge, Shibuya Crossing, Meiji
  Jingu torii, Imperial Palace and Shinjuku Twin Towers.
- Road-first plan: authored rings and radials, 15 district grids at their own angles, 805 road edges,
  6 bridges, 69 block faces and 689 parcels.
- Ten ordinary building families, capped by footprint and seeded by district.
- Green Yamanote loop with four stopping trains; 64 cars, 4 boats and 218 pedestrians.
- Day/night city with neon signs, lit windows, floodlit towers and bridge lights.
- English and Japanese UI, 345 × 345 embed, snapshots, auto-rotate, camera flights.
- Single-file offline build; 30 deterministic browser checks and plan/geometry tests.
